Civil and Allied Department

The Civil and Allied Department is committed to developing professionals in engineering, technology, and applied sciences. With a strong emphasis on civil engineering, environmental, and chemical technology disciplines, the department prepares students to address industry challenges through innovation, sustainability, and technical proficiency
