Electrical and AlliedDepartment

The Electrical and Allied Department is dedicated to advancing knowledge and skills in electrical, electronics, and allied technologies. With strong flagship programs in engineering and a diverse range of technology-focused disciplines, the department equips students with the technical expertise and innovative mindset needed to thrive in rapidly evolving industries.

Engr. Raymund M. Lozada, PECE

Department Head
