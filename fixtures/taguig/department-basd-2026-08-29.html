Basic Arts and Sciences Department

The Basic Arts and Sciences Department plays a vital role in providing foundational knowledge and skills in various disciplines. Beyond offering specialized programs in technical-vocational education, it also serves as a service department, delivering essential courses in Mathematics, Sciences, Arts, and Languages.

Dr. Heherson P. Ramos, LPT
